## Word Selection Priority by Level

### N5 (Absolute beginner)
- Numbers, days, months
- Family members
- Basic verbs (食べる、飲む、行く、来る、見る)
- Basic adjectives (大きい、小さい、いい、悪い)
- Classroom/school vocabulary
- Self-introduction words

### N4 (Elementary)
- Extended daily life (cooking, cleaning, shopping)
- Travel vocabulary
- Emotions and opinions
- Basic work vocabulary
- Compound verbs (持っていく、出かける)
- Time expressions (先日、来週、毎月)

### N3 (Intermediate)
- Abstract concepts (経験、影響、関係)
- News vocabulary
- Formal expressions
- Business basics
- Compound words (取り組む、見直す)
- Onomatopoeia basics (ゆっくり、はっきり)

### N2 (Upper-intermediate)
- Academic/formal language
- Specialized vocabulary (法律、経済、科学)
- Literary expressions
- Idioms and four-character compounds
- Nuanced synonyms
- Written language vs spoken

### N1 (Advanced)
- Classical/literary vocabulary
- Specialized technical terms
- Rare readings of common kanji
- Archaic but still-used expressions
- Professional/academic register
